About Me

My name is Emma Stewart, and I am currently a Ph.D. student in Digital History at Clemson University. I earned both my B.A. and M.A. in History from the University of Central Oklahoma, where I also minored in Women’s, Gender, and Sexuality Studies. My M.A. thesis, “Back to the Land of Exclusion: A History of Lesbian Separatism, Community, and Trans Marginalization in the United States, 1970s–2020s,” passed with distinction.

My research focuses on narratives of women and gender in the twentieth-century United States with an emphasis on feminist movements. Engaging both feminist and queer historiography, my work examines how these radical communities fit into broader U.S. activist movements. Themes of rurality, alternative community, and gender identity exist at the center of my work.
