About Me

My name is Emma Stewart, and I am currently a Ph.D. student in Digital History at Clemson University. I earned both my B.A. and M.A. in History from the University of Central Oklahoma, where my MA thesis passed with distinction. My areas of focus are U.S. women's history and digital methods. Themes of rurality, alternative community, utopianism, and gender intersect at the center of my work.
