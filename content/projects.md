### Northwest Arkansas Women's Oral History Project

### Herland Archive Digitization
<div style="display: flex; gap: 20px; align-items: flex-start;">

<img src="/images/herland-arch.png" alt="Herland Archive" width="200" style="flex-shrink: 0;">

<div>
From 2022-2025, I worked on a project funded by a National Endowment for the Humanities grant to digitize the Herland Archive's rare collection of feminist periodicals housed at the University of Central Oklahoma's Women's Research Center and BGLTQ+ Student Center. I was one of a handful of students who scanned and created metadata for over 40,000 pages of the collection. The entire process introduced me to the labor behind digitization. I learned how to scan documents, compress files, and run OCR to create text-searchable PDFs suitable for public access. The digital archive is housed on the ShareOK website and can be accessed <a href="https://hdl.handle.net/11244/340059">here</a>.
